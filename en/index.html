FAQ

Which apps work with RealTTS?

RealTTS works with common meeting, messaging and voice tools including WeChat, WhatsApp, Telegram, Skype, LINE and Zoom.

Does the client need to install it?

No. You just turn on RealTTS on your side. Your client keeps using their usual apps—WeChat, WhatsApp and the like—with nothing to install.

Can I export meeting content?

Yes. Both original and translated text appear live and can be exported after the meeting.

Why are headphones recommended?

Headphones reduce speaker echo entering the microphone, making recognition and translation more stable and clear.

How do one-click presets work?

RealTTS lets you save your go-to settings as presets. Store your preferred language pair, voice, speed, and more, then switch scenarios instantly with one click — no need to reconfigure every time.

Which languages are supported?

RealTTS supports Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, Russian, Spanish, Portuguese and Arabic — 9 common business languages covering major global trade markets, with more on the way.

Will the translation still sound like me?

Yes. With a custom voice profile, translated speech keeps your familiar tone and personality instead of sounding like a generic machine voice.

Is there any delay that disrupts the conversation?

Response time is in seconds and two-way translation is almost simultaneous, so the conversation flows naturally and your client never feels they are waiting for a translation.

What scenarios is RealTTS for?

It fits every stage of cross-border business: real-time chats on WeChat, WhatsApp and LINE, video meetings on Zoom, Teams and Skype, face-to-face talks, trade-show booth reception, and on-the-go mobile interpretation — from client negotiation and quoting to customer service.

How is RealTTS priced?

Monthly, quarterly and annual plans are available; quarterly and annual billing offer better value for long-term use.
